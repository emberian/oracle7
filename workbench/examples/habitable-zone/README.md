# Habitable zone (example)

A worked example of a Workbench specialist built from one published paper: where liquid water could last on a rocky
planet's surface around a given star, from Kopparapu et al. 2013 (ApJ 765, 131, with its erratum). It answers the
zone's edges for a star of given temperature and luminosity, and whether a planet at a given distance (or receiving a
given share of Earth's sunlight) is inside it. It declines stars outside the 2,600-7,200 K range the formulas were fitted on.
Copy this folder into `workbench/local/specialists/` to try the exam, or press "Add the example" on the Build page.
